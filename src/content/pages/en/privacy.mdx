## What is collected

This site is statically generated and sets no cookies of its own. If the newsletter or
contact form is connected to a provider, that provider receives the details you submit.

## How it is used

Submitted details are used only to reply to you or to send the emails you asked for. They
are not sold or shared for advertising.

## Your reading preference

The light or dark reading mode you pick is stored in your browser's local storage under

<code>monograph-theme</code>. It never leaves your device and you can clear it at any time
